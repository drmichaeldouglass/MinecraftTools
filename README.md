# 🪄 Helper Tools: a Minecraft Bedrock Add-On

**Current version: 1.5.0** · Requires Minecraft Bedrock **1.26.40 or newer** · No experiments needed

Helper Tools gives every player a **Helper Device**, a glowing magic wand. Use it and a colourful menu opens with one-tap helpers: change the time or weather, heal up, escape from a cave, gain superpowers, summon tame pets, teleport to friends or biomes, and build anything from a simple hole to a whole furnished village, a glowing beacon, a sphere or a swimming pool. Builder tools show a glowing 3D preview in the world before anything changes, and the last build can be undone.

It was made with younger players in mind, so every option is a big labelled button and nothing needs typing in chat. It works just as well for grown-ups who want quick building and travel tools.

### 🎮 Game Modes & Time
- ☀️ **Set Daytime** and 🌙 **Set Nighttime**
- 🌤️ **Clear Weather**: stops rain, snow and thunderstorms.
- 🎨 **Creative Mode** and ⚔️ **Survival Mode**: switch your own game mode.

### 🛡️ Life Savers & Rescue
- ❤️ **Instant Heal & Feed**: fills your health and hunger bars.
- 🛡️ **Monster Begone!**: removes hostile mobs (zombies, creepers, skeletons, spiders, phantoms, endermen, witches, slimes and more) within 35 blocks. Animals and pets are left alone.
- ⬆️ **Escape to Surface**: finds open sky above you and lifts you out of a cave or ravine.
- 🔥 **Lava & Fire Shield**: puts out fire, nudges you up out of danger and gives 2 minutes of Fire Resistance.
- 🥛 **Cure All Effects**: clears every potion effect, like drinking milk.

### ⚡ Superpowers & Flying
- 👀 **Night Vision** for 15 minutes.
- 🏃 **Super Speed & Jump**: Speed II and Jump Boost II for 5 minutes.
- 🪽 **Elytra & Flight Kit**: an Elytra and 64 firework rockets.
- ⭐ **Gain 30 XP Levels**: experience to spend on enchanting.

### 📍 Spawn & Teleport
- 📍 **Set Spawn Point Here** and 🚀 **Teleport to Spawn Point**
- 👥 **Teleport to Another Player**: pick anyone online from a list.
- 🌎 **Find Nearest Biome**: choose a biome in your current dimension (Overworld, Nether or The End). The add-on shows its coordinates and distance, and **🚀 Teleport There Now** takes you straight there.
- 🏰 **Find Nearest Structure**: choose a village, trial chamber, ancient city, stronghold, fortress, End city and more. The add-on gives you the `/locate` command to find it (see [Finding structures](#finding-structures) below).
- 🧭 **Teleport to Coordinates**: type X and Z (Y is optional) and go there.
- 💀 **Back to Where I Died**: takes you to your last death spot, in any dimension, so you can pick up your things. Dropped items vanish after 5 minutes.
- 🗺️ **Waypoints**: save up to 20 named places (Home, Mine, Village...) and travel back to any of them, in any dimension.

Every teleport loads the destination first and puts you on solid ground with headroom, never in lava, inside rock or on the Nether roof. You get 10 seconds of Resistance and Fire Resistance for the landing. If there is nowhere safe to stand, you stay where you are.

### 🧰 Handy Helpers
- 💎 **Ore Detector**: pick an ore (diamonds, ancient debris, emeralds, gold, iron, redstone, lapis, copper, coal or quartz) and a distance of 8 to 32 blocks. It counts the ore blocks around you, says how far away the closest one is and which way (for example "4 east, 9 down"), and draws a trail of green sparkles towards it for 15 seconds.
- 👻 **Show Monster Spawn Spots**: marks pitch-dark floor within 16 blocks where monsters can spawn with grey clouds for 30 seconds. It is made for caves and buildings: open ground under the sky is dark only at night, so it is not marked.
- 🧭 **Coordinates & Chunk Borders**: turns the on-screen coordinates on or off (for everyone in the world), or outlines the 16 × 16 chunk you are in for 60 seconds.
- ⛏️ **Mining & Farming Powers**: switch each one on or off for yourself. **Tree Feller** fells a whole natural tree when you chop a log with an axe (log cabins are safe: it only works when natural leaves touch the trunk). **Vein Miner** breaks the rest of an ore vein when you mine one block with a pickaxe. **Auto-Replant** replants ripe wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroot and nether wart using seeds from your inventory. Sneak while breaking a block to break just that one. Extra blocks drop as if mined without enchantments, and your tool wears as if it broke each block.

### 🐾 Pets & Companions
- 🐕 Tamed dog, 🐈 tamed cat, 🦜 tamed parrot, 🐴 tamed horse with a saddle, 🦾 iron golem bodyguard and 🦎 axolotl.

### ✨ Enchant & Armour
- ✨ **Enchant an Item**: pick a tool, weapon or piece of armour from your hotbar, inventory, armour slots or off hand (you keep holding the Helper Device), then pick from the enchantments it can take and a level up to the normal maximum, or add every compatible enchantment at its top level in one go.
- 🛡️ **Full Armour Set**: leather, chainmail, iron, gold, diamond or netherite, optionally enchanted (Protection IV, Unbreaking III, Mending, Respiration, Aqua Affinity, Thorns, Swift Sneak, Feather Falling IV, Depth Strider) and equipped into empty armour slots. Existing armour and Elytra stay equipped; the remaining pieces go into your inventory, or drop at your feet if it is full.
- ⚒️ **Enchanted Tool Kit**: sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel and bow in your chosen material, optionally enchanted (Sharpness V, Looting III, Efficiency V, Fortune III, Silk Touch, Power V, Infinity, Unbreaking III, Mending).
- 🎨 **Armour Trim Kit**: choose one of the 18 trim patterns and a trim material (iron, copper, gold, lapis, emerald, diamond, netherite, redstone, amethyst, quartz or resin). Minecraft gives add-ons no way to apply a trim directly, so the device hands you the smithing templates, the material and a smithing table: put a template, an armour piece and the material in the table's three slots to apply it.

## 📥 How to install

The ready-made installers are in the [`dist`](dist) folder of this repository:

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **`HelperTools.mcaddon`** | Both packs in one file. **Use this one.** |
| `HelperTools_BP.mcpack` | Behavior pack only |
| `HelperTools_RP.mcpack` | Resource pack only |

To download a file from GitHub, open it in the `dist` folder and press the **Download raw file** button (the down arrow at the top right of the file view).

### 🪟 Windows
1. Download `HelperTools.mcaddon`.
2. Double-click it. Minecraft opens and shows "Successfully imported" for both packs.

### 📱 iPhone and iPad
1. Download `HelperTools.mcaddon` in Safari, or AirDrop it from a Mac, or save it to iCloud Drive.
2. Open the **Files** app and tap the file. If it doesn't open straight away, tap the **Share** button and choose **Minecraft**.
3. Minecraft opens and imports both packs.

### 🤖 Android
1. Download `HelperTools.mcaddon`.
2. Open your Files app, tap the file and choose **Minecraft**.

### 🎮 Consoles (Xbox, PlayStation, Switch)
Consoles can't import add-on files directly. Install the add-on on a PC, phone or tablet, create a world with it there, then play that world on the console using **Realms** or by joining the host over LAN or as a friend.

### 🔄 Updating to a new version
Import the new `HelperTools.mcaddon` the same way. Minecraft replaces the old version, and worlds that already have the add-on use the new one the next time they load.

## ⚙️ World setup

Do this when you create a new world, or under **Edit** for an existing world.

1. **Game settings**
   - Turn **Cheats** ON. The chat commands below (`/scriptevent` and `/locate`) need it.
   - Leave all **Experiments** OFF. Helper Tools uses the stable Script API, so Beta APIs is not needed.
2. **Behavior Packs** ➔ **Available** ➔ select **Helper Tools (Behavior Pack)** ➔ **Activate**. Minecraft offers to add the matching resource pack too; say yes.
3. Check **Resource Packs** ➔ **Active** lists **Helper Tools (Resource Pack)**. If it doesn't, activate it from **Available**.
4. Press **Create** or **Play**.

> 💾 Builder tools change a lot of blocks at once. Try them in a test world first, and make a copy of any world you care about before building in it (**Edit** ➔ **Copy World**).

## 🎮 How to use it

### Opening the menu
You get a Helper Device in your inventory automatically when you join the world. Hold it and use it:

| Device | How to open the menu |
| --- | --- |
| PC | Right-click |
| Phone or tablet | Tap and hold the screen, or tap the **Open Helper Tools** button |
| Controller | Left trigger (`LT`, `L2` or `ZL`) |

Pick a section, then a tool. Every sub-menu has a **⬅️ Back** button.

### Building with the 3D preview
1. Stand where you want the build to start and face the direction you want it to go.
2. Open **🏗️ Builder Tools**, choose a tool and set the sliders and options.
3. Leave **✨ Show 3D Glowing Preview Guide First** turned on. A glowing outline of the build appears in the world.
4. Walk around to check the size and position. A window asks you to choose **✅ Confirm & Execute** to build or **❌ Cancel** to try again.
5. Made a mistake? Use **↩️ Undo Last Build/Hole**.

About undo:
- It remembers **one** build per player: your most recent dig, fill, flatten, platform, base, house, village, shape, beacon, pool, drain, contraption, bridge, staircase, paste or statue. A new build replaces the saved one.
- The undo is kept in memory, so it is lost when the world is closed.
- Torches from **Light Up Area** and things removed by **Area Cleanup** can't be undone.

For the **Modern Village** or **Modern City**, find a reasonably open area, leave **Flatten and prepare the ground first** on unless the land is already flat, and wait a few seconds while it builds in stages. In the city, every tower has a lift core behind the lobby: open a lift door, step in and the bubble column carries you up (left shaft) or down (right shaft); open the door on the storey you want and step out.

### Furniture
Houses, the village and the city are furnished with the add-on's own furniture blocks: chairs, sofas, tables, floor lamps (they give off light), televisions, fridges and computers. You can also place them yourself from the Construction tab of the Creative inventory; they turn to face you when placed. If you would rather use plain Minecraft blocks (stairs for seats, fences for tables), turn off **Use Helper Tools furniture** in the house or village form.

Shapes rest on the ground at your feet and start one block in front of you, so stand back from where you want the middle to be. Large hollow spheres and cones are placed over a few seconds. A **Beacon** only shines when nothing solid is straight above it, so leave **Clear the sky** on unless you are already outdoors. After it is built, tap the beacon block to pick its power.

The **Spawner XP Farm** is ready as soon as it is built: the zombie or skeleton spawner you picked is already set and sealed inside the dark room. Stand at the half-block gap at the front and hit the mobs' legs. Drops collect in the recessed chest beside the gap. Hostile mobs need a difficulty other than Peaceful, and you must stay within 16 blocks of the spawner. The **Flood Wheat Farm** is harvested by pressing the button at the back; press it again to take the water back, then replant.

The **Swimming Pool** is dug into the ground in front of you, so stand on flat land at the level you want the deck. **Drain Water & Lava** clears a cube around you; water from outside the cube can flow back in, so drain a little wider than the pond you want gone.

**Redstone Contraptions** are placed in front of you like other builds. For the Bubble Elevator, open the door at the bottom, step in and float up; at the top, walk out onto the landing behind the tower, and use the magma shaft (if you added one) to come down, stepping out of its door quickly because magma is hot. The Firework Launcher fires when you press the button on the end of the row. The Sugar Cane Farm harvests by itself; collect from the chest at the left-hand end of the water channel. The Spawner XP Farm's spawner is already set and sealed in the dark room, and the mobs it makes are washed down to the gap at the front. The Wool Farm's sheep needs the grass around it to keep growing back, so leave the pen floor as grass. The Railway comes with a minecart: place it on the first rail, climb in and push forward. The Pumpkin & Melon Farm's stems take a while to grow; ripe fruit is knocked off by the pistons and ends up in the chest in the pit at the right-hand end. The Egg Collector's chickens lay into the chest at the front. The Kelp Farm's chests are at both ends; smelt kelp into dried kelp to eat, or craft dried kelp blocks for fuel. At the Fishing Spot, stand on the seat, face the pool and fish as normal; when your inventory is full, catches fall into the chest under the seat. Press the Doorbell's button to hear its tune. The Secret Door starts open: flip the lever in the nook beside the doorway (there is one on each side) to slide the wall shut, and flip it back to open it. In the Trading Hall, trade with each villager through the window above its counter; its workstation is behind it so it can restock.

### Copy and paste
Open **🏗️ Builder Tools** ➔ **📋 Copy & Paste**. Stand on the ground at one corner of a build and choose **Mark Corner 1**, walk to the opposite corner and choose **Mark Corner 2**, then **Copy Between the Corners** and choose how tall the copy should be. The copy includes the ground you marked. Stand where you want it and choose **Paste in Front of Me**; you can turn it a quarter, half or three-quarter turn. Your copy is saved in the world, so it is still there next time. **↩️ Undo Last Build** takes a paste away.

### Statues
**🗿 Place a Statue** places one of the statues that come with the add-on, in front of you. Empty space around a statue leaves the ground alone. To make your own from a photogrammetry scan, a Blender model or a picture, see [`tools/README.md`](tools/README.md).

### Finding biomes
Open **📍 Spawn & Teleport** ➔ **🌎 Find Nearest Biome**, pick a biome, and press **🚀 Teleport There Now** in the result window. Choose **✅ Done** instead to just keep the coordinates. The search uses the world seed, so places you have changed may look different from how they were generated. Underground biomes such as Lush Caves and Deep Dark land you near the depth where the biome was found.

### Finding structures
Minecraft doesn't let add-ons read where structures are, so this takes two steps:

1. Open **📍 Spawn & Teleport** ➔ **🏰 Find Nearest Structure** and pick one. The add-on closes the menu and shows the command, for example `/locate structure minecraft:village`, and Minecraft replies in chat with something like `The nearest village is at block 1256, (y?), -648`.
2. Open **🧭 Teleport to Coordinates** and type the X and Z numbers in. You can also paste the whole "The nearest ..." line into the X box and leave Y and Z empty; the add-on picks the numbers out.

Typing `/locate` yourself needs Cheats on and operator rights.

### Chat commands
These work with Cheats on. They are handy if you lose your device or prefer typing.

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/scriptevent tools:device` | Gives you a Helper Device if you don't have one |
| `/scriptevent tools:goto 1256 -648` | Teleports to X 1256, Z -648 and lands on the surface |
| `/scriptevent tools:goto 1256 70 -648` | Teleports to X, Y, Z |

You can also get a spare device from the main menu with **🎁 Get Extra Helper Device**.

### Multiplayer
Every player gets their own Helper Device and their own undo. **👥 Teleport to Another Player** lists everyone online. Time and weather changes affect everyone in the world; game mode changes only affect you.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| The device didn't appear | Run `/scriptevent tools:device`, or rejoin the world. Check the behavior pack is active in the world settings. |
| The device looks like a purple and black square | The resource pack isn't active. Activate **Helper Tools (Resource Pack)** under Resource Packs. |
| Nothing happens when I use the device | Make sure your Minecraft is 1.26.40 or newer. Older versions can't run the add-on's scripts. |
| "Nothing to undo!" | Undo only covers your most recent build, and only until the world is closed. |
| A biome isn't found | It may be further away than the search reaches (about 4,000 blocks in each direction), or not exist in that dimension. |
| `/locate` says unknown command or no permission | Turn Cheats on and make sure you are an operator. |

## 🧑‍💻 For developers

### Repository layout

| Path | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `Behavior_Pack/` | Behavior pack: manifest, the Helper Device item, the furniture block definitions in `blocks/` and `scripts/main.js` |
| `Resource_Pack/` | Resource pack: manifest, textures, furniture block models in `models/blocks/` and language files |
| `Behavior_Pack/structures/` | Preset spawner structures (`helpertools/`) and statues (`helpertools_statues/`) |
| `tests/regression.cjs` | Regression tests that run against a mocked Script API |
| `tools/` | Generators for the spawner and statue structures, with their own tests and [`README.md`](tools/README.md) |
| `build.sh` | Packages both packs into the installers |
| `dist/` | Built installers, committed so players can download them from the repository |
| `CHANGELOG.md` | Release notes for each version |

The add-on uses `@minecraft/server` 2.9.0 and `@minecraft/server-ui` 2.0.0.

### Testing and building

```bash
node tests/regression.cjs   # run the regression tests
python3 -m unittest discover -s tools -p 'test_*.py'   # statue tool tests
bash build.sh               # rebuild dist/HelperTools.mcaddon and both .mcpack files
```

`build.sh` needs `zip` on your PATH. Always rebuild and commit `dist/` together with any change to the packs. The tests mock the Script API rather than running Minecraft, so check changes in a disposable world before release.

### Releasing an update
Bump the version in both `Behavior_Pack/manifest.json` and `Resource_Pack/manifest.json`, keeping the header version, every module version and the behavior pack's dependency on the resource pack the same. Then run the tests, rebuild, and add an entry to [`CHANGELOG.md`](CHANGELOG.md).

See [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md) for the full contributor checklist.
